Pocket Photographer Account & data

Delete your account

You can delete your account at any time. This page explains how, what is removed, and what we keep.

In the app

  • Open Pocket Photographer and go to Profile.
  • Tap Delete account, read the summary and confirm.
  • If you have a booking that is not finished yet, cancel it or finish it first, so that no payment is left hanging. Then delete your account.

If you cannot open the app

Email privacy@pocketphotographer.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm it is you, then delete the account, normally within 30 days.

What is removed

  • Your name, username, profile photo and, for photo buddies, your bio, equipment and experience details.
  • Your sign-in. You are signed out and cannot sign back in with the account.
  • If you never booked, reviewed or applied, the whole account is erased.
  • ID documents and selfies submitted by photo buddy applicants are deleted automatically 30 days after we decide the application.

What we keep

  • Booking, payment and dispute records, which we must keep for accounting and to resolve disputes. They are kept without your name: they show as a deleted user.
  • Reviews you left stay on the photo buddy's profile, without your name.

Questions

Write to privacy@pocketphotographer.app. See also our Privacy Policy.
