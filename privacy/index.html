Privacy policy

Pocket Photographer connects travellers and locals with casual local photo buddies in Amsterdam, Lisbon and Paris. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it — in plain language, not legal filler.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email, display name, @username, and country if you add one.
  • Booking details: the city, date, time, meeting point, add-ons and price you choose or receive.
  • Messages and photos exchanged through the app for a session — sent between you and your photo buddy only.
  • Safety information you choose to give us: reports, disputes, or emergency-contact details for a live session.
  • Waitlist sign-ups on our website: your name, email, the city you live in, whether you're interested in booking or becoming a photo buddy, and which link or platform you came from.
  • Basic technical data (device type, app version) and a record of key booking steps (for example a request, a delivery or a cancellation), tied to your account, to keep the app working and see where people get stuck.

Payment data

Payment is handled by our payment processor, Stripe. Pocket Photographer never sees or stores your full card number — only what the processor gives us to run the app, like the card's last four digits, its brand, and whether a payment succeeded.

Why we use it

  • To run the booking, chat, and delivery flow you're actually using.
  • To keep meetups safe — the meetup-code check, emergency screen, and dispute process.
  • To respond when you contact us or report a problem.

We don't use your data for ad targeting, and we don't sell it to anyone.

Who sees it

  • Your photo buddy or customer sees only what's needed to complete a booking — your name and meeting details, not your account email.
  • Supabase, our database and hosting provider, stores the data on our behalf as a data processor — it doesn't use it for its own purposes.
  • PostHog, our product analytics provider (EU region), receives your account ID (not your email) and the booking steps described above, on our behalf as a data processor.
  • Formspree, the service behind our website's waitlist form, receives the waitlist details and emails them to us.
  • We share information with authorities only if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and booking history while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data, aside from what we're legally required to retain (for example, records tied to an open dispute or safety report). Waitlist details are kept until we've told you your city is open, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

  • Ask what data we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal data.
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use your data.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority (for example the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens in the Netherlands, CNPD in Portugal, or the CNIL in France) if you think we've got something wrong.

To use any of these, email privacy@pocketphotographer.app — we aim to reply within 30 days.

Age

Pocket Photographer is for adults. You need to be 18 or older to book or offer a session — this is a safety meetup service, not a general app.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to the database is restricted, and every write to your data goes through checks tied to your account — nobody, including us, can act as you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll show you a notice in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy: privacy@pocketphotographer.app. For anything else, use Send feedback from your profile in the app.
