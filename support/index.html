Pocket Photographer Help & support

Help & support

Answers to the most common questions, and how to reach a person if something is wrong.

Contact us

Email hello@pocketphotographer.app. We read everything and aim to reply within two working days. For privacy and data requests use privacy@pocketphotographer.app.

If you feel unsafe

Call your local emergency number first (112 works across the EU). In the app, open the Emergency screen from any booking, or report a problem from the booking. We are not monitored live, so never rely on us instead of the emergency services.

Cancellation and refunds

You can cancel any booking before the session starts, from either side, and you get a full refund to your original payment method. Repeatedly cancelling or not showing up may limit your ability to book or accept sessions.

How payment works

The total is your photo buddy's price plus a 15% service fee, shown before you pay. When you send a booking request your card is held, not charged. It is charged only when your photo buddy accepts, and released if they decline or do not reply. Payments are handled by Stripe, and we never see your full card number.

Photos missing or not as agreed

Open the booking in the app and tap Dispute within the window shown there. We look at what both sides say before deciding what is fair.

Report or block someone

Open their profile or your chat and choose Report or Block. Reports go to our safety team. Accounts that break the community guidelines are suspended or removed.

Delete your account

In the app go to Profile, then Delete account. Full details, and what to do if you cannot open the app, are on the delete your account page.
